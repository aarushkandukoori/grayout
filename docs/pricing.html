What happens after you subscribe

No surprises. Here is the whole life of a Grayout Pro subscription.

  1. Day 1

    Start the trial

    Checkout is a Stripe page. Monthly plans start with 7 free days for new subscribers. The trial includes up to 1,000 checks. Your card is saved now and first charged on day 8.

  2. Seconds later

    Grayout unlocks

    If you subscribed from the app, it unlocks itself. If you subscribed here, the next page shows your license key to paste into Settings.

  3. Day 8

    The first charge

    $9.99 plus any tax, then every month on the same date. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. Link sends no reminder email for a 7-day trial, so Grayout shows a Mac notification about two days before it ends.

  4. Every month

    10,000 checks included

    A month of work is about 8,000 checks, roughly 4,000 once unchanged screens are skipped. If you pass 10,000, Grayout checks once every 10 minutes until the count resets on the 1st, so it keeps working.

  5. Any time

    Cancel in two clicks

    Use Manage subscription in the menu bar or link.com. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then Grayout stops checking. It never grays your screen over billing.

Who you pay

Grayout Pro is sold through Link, Stripe’s checkout. Stripe is the merchant of record: it works out and collects sales tax or VAT, sends your receipts, and answers payment questions. Your statement shows LINK.COM*, and you can see or cancel the order at link.com. Grayout never sees your card number. Refunds and terms.
