Billing

The awkward questions.

What counts as a check?

One look at your screen. Grayout skips the call when nothing has changed on any display and the frontmost app is the same, so an idle desk costs almost nothing; it forces a real check at least every three minutes regardless.

At the 45-second default, a full working month is typically well under the 15,000 included.

What happens past 15,000 checks?

The interval stretches instead of cutting you off. Nothing goes gray because of a billing state, and there is no overage charge.

What happens when the trial ends?

The monthly plan starts billing $9.99 on day eight. Cancel before then and you are not charged. The app tells you which day you are on.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free taste. For Pro you pay through the checkout page and the app claims its license on its own: there is no password to make and no key to copy.

How do I cancel?

Settings > Manage subscription opens Stripe’s billing portal. Cancel there and your subscription runs to the end of the period you paid for.

What does Grayout see?

A resized screenshot of each display and an optional webcam frame, sent for each check and dropped as soon as the model answers. They are never stored, never logged and never written to disk by the service. The privacy page lists everything.
