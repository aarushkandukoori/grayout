If your screen is stuck gray

In every edition, easiest first

  1. Click Restore color now in the Grayout menu. It works in every state, paused included.
  2. Click I’m working if it is there. Color returns and Grayout backs off for ten minutes.
  3. Quit Grayout. Color comes back at once.
  4. Do nothing. A watchdog restores color after 20 minutes no matter what.

If the menu doesn’t respond, force quit Grayout. It lives in the menu bar, so it usually isn’t listed in the Force Quit window (Option-Command-Esc). Open Activity Monitor, select Grayout, click Stop (the X button), then Force Quit. Or run killall -9 Grayout in Terminal. The gray doesn’t block clicks, so both work while it is on.

Mac App Store edition

The gray is a window that belongs to Grayout, so it closes whenever Grayout stops, force quit included. Grayout never changes a display setting in this edition, so there is nothing to switch off in System Settings.

Direct download (GitHub or Homebrew)

The gray is macOS’s Color Filters setting, which outlives a force quit. Relaunch Grayout: every launch turns grayscale off before anything else. With no Terminal: System Settings > Accessibility > Display > Color Filters, and switch it off. That is the switch the direct download uses. The Terminal command is on the download page.

Setup and permissions

  • Asking first - Grayout lists what it sends at each check and who receives it, and captures nothing until you click Allow and continue. If you chose Not now, or turned off Send screenshots for checks in Settings, checks stay off until you allow them again.
  • Screen Recording - the one permission Grayout needs. Turn Grayout on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording, then relaunch it; macOS applies the permission only after a restart. Step by step.
  • The first-launch warning - direct download only, because it is not notarized yet. The three clicks. Apps from the Mac App Store open without it.
  • The camera - optional and off by default. Turn it off in Settings, or in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.

Your data

What Grayout sends, to whom, and what is kept is all in the privacy policy. On your Mac, Delete all history in Settings empties the log, and Erase this Mac’s Grayout data removes everything Grayout keeps there. To have what the service holds deleted, see retention and deletion. The terms of service cover the Grayout service and Grayout Pro.
