Next

Three things worth knowing.

It lives in the menu bar

No Dock icon. Every control is one click away, including Restore color now.

Billing is in the app

Settings > Manage subscription opens Stripe’s billing portal, where you can change plan or cancel.

You can always quit

Quitting restores color on the way out, and a watchdog restores it after 20 minutes no matter what.
