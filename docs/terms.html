Who and what

Grayout is made by Aarush Kandukoori (“we”). These terms cover the Grayout service and the Grayout Pro subscription. The Grayout app itself is open source under the MIT license, which governs the code; these terms govern what we run and what we sell.

Grayout is a macOS app that checks your screen with an AI model at regular intervals and, when it is confident you are clearly off task, turns your display gray with a red border until you get back to work. It does not block, close or lock anything.

What you get

  • Your own key: the app is free. You bring an OpenAI or Anthropic API key and pay your provider directly. These terms only matter here for what this website says.
  • Free taste: 100 checks on the Grayout service per device, with no card and no account. Free checks are also limited per network and per day across everyone, so they can pause on a busy day.
  • Grayout Pro: $9.99 a month or $79 a year, plus tax where it applies, for up to 10,000 checks a month. Past 10,000, the service slows to one check every 10 minutes until the count resets at 00:00 UTC on the 1st. A license key unlocks the app and can be used on your own Macs.

Trials, renewals and cancelling

  • The monthly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. A card is needed to start it. If you don’t cancel during the trial, you are charged at the end of it.
  • The trial includes up to 1,000 checks. A week of work is about 1,900 checks, roughly 950 once unchanged screens are skipped, which Grayout does on its own. Past 1,000, the service slows to one check every 10 minutes until the trial ends.
  • One free trial per person. If the email address or card you check out with already had a Grayout trial, the new trial ends at once and the first month is charged right away.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically each month or year until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time from Manage subscription in the app, from the billing portal, or at link.com. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • Refunds are covered by the refund policy.
  • If we ever change prices, the new price is posted here and applies to you only from a renewal after the change.

Acceptable use

  • Use Grayout on your own Mac, to watch your own screen. Don’t use it to monitor anyone else, and don’t install it on a computer you don’t own or on someone else’s account.
  • Use the service through the Grayout app. Don’t script it, resell it, or use it as a general image-recognition API.
  • Don’t share your license key publicly or try to get around the free limits, rate limits or the monthly allowance.
  • Don’t send content that is illegal for you to send.

We may suspend a license or device that breaks these rules. A refunded or disputed subscription ends its license.

What Grayout can and can’t promise

Grayout is a nudge. It uses an AI model, and models make wrong calls in both directions: it may miss a distraction or flag real work. When anything goes wrong (an error, an unsure answer, a service outage, a billing problem) it counts the check as on task, so a fault never grays your screen. Color always comes back with Restore color now, by quitting, or after 20 minutes on its own.

The service may be unavailable at times. We will try to keep it running, and we don’t promise any particular uptime.
