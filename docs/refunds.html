Refunds

If Grayout Pro isn’t worth it to you, you shouldn’t pay for it. Here is exactly how refunds work.

Effective 2026-10-15.

30 days, no argument

Ask within 30 days of any charge, monthly or yearly, and it is refunded in full.

Yearly, after that

After the first 30 days of a yearly plan, you get the unused whole months back, prorated.

Through Link

Link support handles refund requests for 60 days after a charge.

The trial is free

Cancel during the 7-day trial and you are never charged, so there is nothing to refund.

How to ask

  • Email from the address you paid with. Say which charge, and that you want a refund. No reason needed.
  • Or contactContact Link support at link.com, where the purchase is listed as sold through Link. Stripe can refund purchases made through Link within 60 days.

Please don’t post your email address, receipt or license key in a public GitHub issue.

From the Mac App Store?

The app is free there. Grayout Pro is bought from Stripe on the web, so Apple can’t refund it and it won’t appear at reportaproblem.apple.com. Ask us or Link as described above.

What happens next

  • A full refund includes any tax you paid. It goes back to the card you used, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. Link sends the refund notice.
  • A full refund also cancels the subscription and ends its license, so Grayout stops running checks on Pro. Your Mac keeps its settings and its log.
  • A prorated refund on a yearly plan also cancels the subscription, so it does not renew.

Disputes

Please ask for a refund before filing a dispute with your bank. A refund is faster for you, and a dispute ends the license immediately. Stripe reviews every dispute on its side.
