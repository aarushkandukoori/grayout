Manage your subscription

Switch plans, update your card, see receipts, or cancel. Grayout Pro is sold through Link, Stripe’s checkout, so you have more than one way in.

All set. You can close this tab and go back to Grayout.

Subscriptions aren’t open yet

There is nothing to manage here yet. Today’s release runs on your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, which you manage, and can revoke, in your provider’s console. More on your own key.

Subscriptions open soon

Nobody can subscribe to Grayout Pro yet, so there is nothing to manage. The free checks need no account. Once subscriptions open, these are the ways to manage one.

From the menu bar

Click the Grayout icon, then Manage subscription. Stripe’s billing portal opens, already signed in to your account.

At link.com

Sign in with the email you used at checkout. The purchase is listed as sold through Link, and you can cancel, switch plans or update your card there.

Open link.com

Billing portal by email

Enter the email you paid with and Stripe sends you a one-time sign-in link to the billing portal.

Open the billing portal

Lost your license key?

Email from the address you paid with and we’ll send it again. Never post a key or an email address publicly.

If Grayout on your Mac already has it, it keeps working. On a new Mac, ask on GitHub without posting your email or key, and we’ll find a private way to verify.

With your license key

The key goes to the Grayout service in the body of one request, so it can open your Stripe billing portal. It is never put in a link and never saved in this browser.

Good to know

  • Canceling stops the next charge. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, then Grayout stops checking. It never grays your screen over billing.
  • Switching between monthly and yearly is prorated, so you only pay the difference.
  • Receipts come from Link. Your card statement shows LINK.COM*.
  • Refunds within 30 days, no argument. See the refund policy.
  • Two Macs can share one license: paste the same key in Settings > Your plan and click Activate. In the Mac App Store edition, click Connect an existing subscription first.
  • Got Grayout from the Mac App Store? Your subscription isn’t listed under your Apple Account’s subscriptions, because it was bought through Stripe. Manage it from the menu bar or at link.com, never through Apple.
