How Grayout works

Every 45 seconds, Grayout looks at your screen and asks Claude (or GPT) one question: is this person clearly not working?

Two yeses in a row, and your whole Mac goes gray with a red border.

Color comes back the moment you get back to work.

What leaves your Mac. A screenshot of each display, resized, goes from this Mac to Anthropic's or OpenAI's API under your own key, whichever key you enter. Nothing goes to us. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Screenshots are deleted from this Mac right after each check.

Read the full privacy page

It never blocks, closes, or locks anything. Quit is one click in the menu bar.

Grayout needs to live in your Applications folder to remember its permissions and to start at login.

Allow Screen Recording

Grayout can't do anything until macOS lets it see the screen.

Screen Recording Not allowed
  1. Click Open System Settings.
    macOS applies Screen Recording only after the app restarts. Grayout will reopen right here.
  2. Turn on Grayout in the list. macOS may ask you to quit and reopen the app.
  3. Come back here.

macOS thinks Grayout is allowed, but captures come back empty. This happens after an update. Fix: turn Grayout off and on in the list, or paste this in Terminal:

tccutil reset ScreenCapture com.aarushkandukoori.grayout
